Choosing Your Watch

  • Start with your style: choose a decorative bracelet, a floral design, or a sporty band.
  • Check the fit: consider the case size, band length, and how the watch feels on your wrist.
  • Compare smart features: confirm phone compatibility and the functions included with the exact model.
  • Ask about care: check water resistance and cleaning guidance before exposing a watch to water.
